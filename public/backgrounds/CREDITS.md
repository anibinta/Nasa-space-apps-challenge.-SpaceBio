# SpaceBio Background Imagery Credits (NASA-inspired)

All background imagery used in SpaceBio header banners and empty canvas space is NASA-inspired archival/observatory visual material in compressed WebP format (< 300 KB per file). No NASA official logo or insignia is displayed anywhere in the application, and no endorsement is implied.

## Space Mode (`public/backgrounds/space/`)
- `public/backgrounds/space/nebula-01.webp` — NASA-inspired emission nebula deep-space photography (Hubble/JWST archival style; public imagery catalog reference: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/multimedia/hubble-images/).
- `public/backgrounds/space/galaxy-01.webp` — NASA-inspired spiral galaxy starfield vista (public imagery catalog reference: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/multimedia/images/).
- `public/backgrounds/space/deep-field-01.webp` — NASA-inspired deep-space galaxy cluster field (public imagery catalog reference: https://images.nasa.gov/).

## Earth Mode (`public/backgrounds/earth/`)
- `public/backgrounds/earth/earth-limb-01.webp` — NASA-inspired orbital blue atmospheric Earth limb vista (public imagery catalog reference: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/).
- `public/backgrounds/earth/earth-horizon-01.webp` — NASA-inspired low-Earth-orbit horizon & cloud bands (public imagery catalog reference: https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/).
- `public/backgrounds/earth/earth-coastline-01.webp` — NASA-inspired daylight continents and coastal ocean vista (public imagery catalog reference: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/topic/earth-from-orbit).
- `public/backgrounds/earth/earth-aurora-01.webp` — NASA-inspired nighttime Earth airglow and aurora ribbon (public imagery catalog reference: https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/Collections/Aurora/).
